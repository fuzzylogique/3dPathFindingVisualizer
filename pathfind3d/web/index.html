3D PATHFINDER

An interactive 3D pathfinding visualizer. Eight search algorithms — A*, Dijkstra, Greedy, three Swarm variants, BFS and DFS — explore a voxel world in true 3D, moving through all 26 neighbouring cells.

Cells can be walls, open ground or weighted terrain that costs more to cross, across cube, prism, pyramid, sphere and wrapping torus worlds. Drag the markers to move the start and goal, build your own obstacles, scrub back through a search, or draw your own route and race the optimum.

Search engine in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly · rendered with Three.js
